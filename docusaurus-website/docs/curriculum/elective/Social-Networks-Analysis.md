**Course syllabus:**

- Graph-theoretic properties of social networks. Game theory in social networks. Information diffusion in social networks. Dynamics of social networks.

- Types of social networks: directed vs. undirected; weighted vs. unweighted; homogeneous vs. heterogeneous networks.

- Network models: Erdős–Rényi random graphs, small-world networks, preferential attachment models.

- Structural analysis of social networks: shortest paths; network diameter and radius; centrality measures.

- Behavioral analysis of social networks: information diffusion; identification of influential nodes.

- Applications of social network analysis in marketing, community detection, recommender systems, and related domains.

- Network visualization.

- Introduction to software tools for social network analysis.
