**Course syllabus:**

This course introduces the principles and practices of managing information technology projects, including both technical and managerial perspectives.

Topics include: project life cycle and process groups; project initiation, planning, execution, monitoring and control, and closing; scope, time, cost, quality, and risk management; stakeholder and communication management; team roles and leadership in IT projects; and an overview of agile and traditional project management methodologies.
