**Course syllabus:**

This course introduces the concepts, technologies, and business models of electronic commerce. Emphasis is placed on understanding how digital technologies support online transactions, digital markets, and network-based business processes.

Topics include: overview of e-commerce and digital business; types of e-commerce (B2C, B2B, C2C, C2B, etc.); e-commerce infrastructure (networks, payment systems, security); digital marketing and customer relationship management; e-business models and value propositions; legal, ethical, and social issues in e-commerce; and trends such as social commerce and mobile commerce.

**References:**

- Turban, E., King, D., Lee, J. K., Liang, T.-P., & Turban, D. C. (2018). Electronic Commerce: A Managerial and Social Networks Perspective (8th ed.). Springer.
