**Course syllabus:**

Introduction to coding theory and error-correcting codes. Topics include: basic notions of codes; Hamming distance; error detection and correction; linear codes; generator and parity-check matrices; syndrome decoding; Hamming codes; cyclic codes; and an introduction to Reed–Solomon and BCH codes.

**References:**

- Huffman, W. C., & Pless, V. (2003). Fundamentals of Error-Correcting Codes. Cambridge University Press.

- MacWilliams, F. J., & Sloane, N. J. A. (1977). The Theory of Error-Correcting Codes. North-Holland.
