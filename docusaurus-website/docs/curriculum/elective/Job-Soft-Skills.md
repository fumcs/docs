**Course syllabus:**

This course focuses on the development of soft skills that are essential for success in the contemporary workplace, particularly in technical and engineering environments.

Topics include: professional communication (oral, written, and online); effective teamwork and collaboration; time and task management; problem solving and critical thinking in professional settings; conflict management and negotiation; ethical conduct and professional responsibility; leadership and followership; intercultural awareness and diversity in the workplace; job search strategies; curriculum vitae and resume preparation; cover letters; and job interview preparation and practice.

The course combines short lectures with interactive activities, role plays, and reflective assignments designed to help students practice and internalize these skills.
