**Course syllabus:**

Introduction to computational intelligence paradigms. Fuzzy methods: basic concepts of fuzzy sets; membership functions; fuzzy operators; fuzzy relations; and fuzzy inference. Evolutionary computation: genetic algorithms and related techniques; representation, selection, crossover, mutation, and fitness evaluation; applications to optimization. Swarm intelligence: basic ideas from ant colony optimization, particle swarm optimization, and similar methods.

Emphasis is placed on understanding how these methods can be applied to optimization, control, and pattern recognition problems, and on comparing their strengths and limitations relative to classical techniques.

**References:**

- Engelbrecht, A. P. (2007). Computational Intelligence: An Introduction (2nd ed.). Wiley.

- Bonabeau, E., Dorigo, M., & Theraulaz, G. (1999). Swarm Intelligence: From Natural to Artificial Systems. Oxford University Press.
