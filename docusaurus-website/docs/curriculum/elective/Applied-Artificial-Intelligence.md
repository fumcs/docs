**Course syllabus:**

This course focuses on practical applications of artificial intelligence in real-world domains. Emphasis is placed on selecting appropriate AI techniques for specific tasks, understanding their limitations, and deploying them responsibly.

Topics include: overview of AI applications in areas such as natural language processing, computer vision, recommendation systems, and decision support; basic workflow of applied AI projects (problem formulation, data preparation, model selection, evaluation, and deployment); use of pre-trained models and APIs; and an introduction to prompt engineering and large language models at an applied level.

Ethical and societal aspects of AI are also discussed, including fairness, accountability, transparency, and the potential impact of AI systems on individuals and organizations.

**Teaching and learning strategies:**

The course is project-oriented, encouraging students to experiment with existing AI tools and frameworks rather than focusing on low-level algorithmic details.
