**Course syllabus:**

Introduction to computer graphics and graphics pipelines; basic geometry and linear algebra for graphics; 2D and 3D transformations; viewing and projection; rasterization; shading and lighting models; texture mapping; basic animation concepts; and an introduction to graphics APIs such as OpenGL or Direct3D.

**References:**

- Foley, J. D., van Dam, A., Feiner, S. K., & Hughes, J. F. (1996). Computer Graphics: Principles and Practice (2nd ed.). Addison-Wesley.

- Akenine-Möller, T., Haines, E., & Hoffman, N. (2018). Real-Time Rendering (4th ed.). CRC Press.
