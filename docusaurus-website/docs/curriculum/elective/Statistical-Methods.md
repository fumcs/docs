**Course syllabus:**

Random samples; sampling distribution of the sample mean; central limit theorem. Point estimation and its properties. Interval estimation for means, variances, and proportions in a single population, and comparison of two populations in a variety of settings: normal and non-normal populations; known and unknown variances; equal and unequal variances; small and large sample sizes; independent and paired samples. Statistical hypothesis testing: basic concepts and principles; types of errors and their importance; test power. Parametric one- and two-sided tests for means, variances, and proportions in a single population, and comparison of two populations under different combinations of known/unknown means and variances, small/large samples, independent and paired samples. Scatter plots; estimation, hypothesis testing, and confidence intervals for the Pearson correlation coefficient. Simple linear regression. Chi-square goodness-of-fit tests. Contingency tables and tests of independence. Data analysis with at least one statistical software package and interpretation of the resulting output.
