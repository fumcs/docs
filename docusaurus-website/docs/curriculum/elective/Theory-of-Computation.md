**Course syllabus:**

- Introduction to models of computation equivalent to the (Turing) computer. Computational models including mathematical models, Turing machines, and the Church–Turing thesis. Gödel numbering and universal Turing machines. Computably enumerable and computable sets. Non-computable sets and creative sets. Oracles. Complexity classes P and NP. Post’s theorem. Brief discussion of more advanced notions of computability. Examples of problems that are not computable by any Turing machine. Proving undecidability using reductions.
