**Course syllabus:**

The aim of this course is to develop students’ ability to read, understand, and produce academic and technical English texts related to computer science. Emphasis is placed on comprehension of textbooks, research articles, technical reports, and documentation, as well as on the accurate use of specialized terminology in speaking and writing. The course also seeks to improve students’ general academic language skills, including summarizing, paraphrasing, and critical reading.

Topics include: basic principles of technical and academic writing; review of grammatical structures that frequently appear in scientific texts; vocabulary and phraseology specific to computer science (such as terms in algorithms, data structures, software engineering, operating systems, networks, and artificial intelligence); reading strategies for technical documents; interpreting tables, figures, and diagrams; writing structured abstracts and short technical reports; and, where appropriate, oral presentation of technical material.

Class activities typically involve guided reading of selected texts, vocabulary and grammar exercises, translation and paraphrasing tasks, and short writing assignments related to computer science topics.
