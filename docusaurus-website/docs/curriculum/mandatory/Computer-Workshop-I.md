**Course syllabus:**

The main topics covered are determined by the department and course instructor so that content appropriate to students’ needs is provided. Some topics that may be used in this course are listed below:

- Ten-finger touch typing

- Introductory familiarity with the Microsoft Office suite

- Familiarity with application services; cloud services such as Dropbox and Google Drive; cloud computing services; and question-and-answer and knowledge-exchange services such as Stack Exchange, Stack Overflow, ChatGPT, and prompt engineering

- Familiarity with the Linux operating system; the concept of the shell; searching within files; permissions; help facilities; compression; and important commands such as `passwd`, `cp`, `ls`, `cd`, `grep`, `su`, `chmod`, and `kill`

- Introductory familiarity with **LaTeX**, basic commands, typesetting, mathematical formula writing, inserting figures, and creating tables
