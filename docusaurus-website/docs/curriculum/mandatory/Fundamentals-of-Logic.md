**Course syllabus:**

The course introduces the basic concepts and techniques of mathematical logic, with an emphasis on applications to computer science.

Topics include: propositional logic, its syntax and semantics; truth tables; logical equivalence; normal forms (CNF, DNF); natural deduction for propositional logic; soundness and completeness of propositional proof systems; Boolean algebra and its relationship to propositional logic; and an introduction to SAT problems and basic SAT algorithms.

First-order (predicate) logic: syntax (terms, formulas, quantifiers), semantics (structures, interpretations, satisfaction); natural deduction rules for first-order logic; soundness and completeness; expressive power and limitations of first-order languages; and brief exposure to definability and model-theoretic ideas at an elementary level.

The course also introduces logic programming at a basic level, for example through the Prolog language: syntax of Horn clauses, simple programs, and their operational semantics.
