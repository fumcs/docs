**Course syllabus:**


- Introduction to artificial intelligence and its historical development; intelligent agents; state space; uninformed search; informed search; A* algorithm and proof of optimality; local search; constraint satisfaction problems; backtracking search; solving CSPs via local search; adversarial search; minimax algorithm and alpha–beta pruning; reinforcement learning; model-based methods; temporal-difference learning and the Q-learning algorithm; introduction to Bayesian networks; representation and conditional independence in Bayesian networks; inference in Bayesian networks, including exact inference and approximate inference via sampling; parameter estimation in Bayesian networks; well-known and practical examples of Bayesian networks such as Markov models, hidden Markov models, and naive Bayes classifiers.

**References:**


- Russell, S., & Norvig, P., (2020), Artificial intelligence: a modern approach, 4th Edition, PEARSON SERIES.
