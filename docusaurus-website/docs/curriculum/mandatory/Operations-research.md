**Course syllabus:**

This course introduces the fundamental concepts and methods of operations research, with an emphasis on formulating and solving optimization models for decision-making in engineering, management, and computer science contexts.

Topics include: modeling decision problems using linear programming; standard form, graphical solution (in low dimensions), and simplex method; duality and sensitivity analysis; modeling and solving transportation, assignment, and network flow problems; integer programming and basic branch-and-bound ideas; introduction to nonlinear programming models and their relation to the dedicated Nonlinear Optimization course.

Additional topics may include queuing models, inventory models, and basic simulation methods, depending on instructor preferences. Practical aspects such as the use of optimization software (e.g., CPLEX, Gurobi, Pyomo, or open-source solvers) are also covered at an introductory level.
