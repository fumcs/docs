**Course syllabus:**

The Internship course is designed to provide students with practical experience in professional environments related to computer science, information technology, or closely allied fields. The main objective is to bridge the gap between academic study and real-world practice by enabling students to observe and participate in authentic work processes.

Internship tasks and responsibilities are defined by the host organization in coordination with the department. Typical activities may include: assisting with software development and testing; participating in data collection, analysis, and reporting; contributing to documentation or technical support; and observing project management and team collaboration practices.

The internship should expose students to organizational culture, professional ethics, communication with colleagues and clients, and the practical constraints under which technical decisions are made.

**Evaluation and supervision:**

The student’s performance is evaluated based on:

- Completion of assigned tasks and responsibilities at the host organization.
- A brief written report describing the work environment, the tasks performed, skills learned, and reflections on the internship experience.
- Feedback from the host organization’s supervisor.
- Final approval by the academic supervisor or designated faculty member.

Further details regarding minimum acceptable activities, documentation, and reporting format are specified in the department’s Internship guidelines.
