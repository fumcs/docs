**Course syllabus:**

Sources and types of numerical error; number representation systems; rounding error and its propagation; conditioning and stability. Root-finding for nonlinear equations and systems (bisection, fixed-point iteration, Newton and secant methods, and related variants). Interpolation and polynomial approximation; least-squares approximation. Numerical differentiation and integration (including Newton–Cotes formulas and Gaussian quadrature). Numerical methods for solving linear systems (direct and iterative methods) and an introduction to error analysis for these methods.
