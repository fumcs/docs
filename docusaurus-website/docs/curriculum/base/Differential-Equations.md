**Course syllabus:**

Basic concepts and definitions; separable and homogeneous differential equations; modeling in other sciences; exact equations and integrating factors; first-order linear equations; first-order nonlinear equations and special cases, including Bernoulli and Riccati equations; applications of first-order equations; second- and higher-order equations and modeling in other sciences; solving second-order equations with constant and variable coefficients; solving inhomogeneous second- and higher-order linear equations; solving the Cauchy–Euler equation and examining several special cases in solving second- and higher-order equations; solving systems of differential equations; solving differential equations using series methods; Gamma function; Frobenius series; Bessel equation; Laplace transform and its applications; introduction to convolution.
