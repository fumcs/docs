# General Mathematics II
## Calculus II
_______________________________________________________________________________
| Bachelor's | Level:       | General Mathematics II                          | Course title:    |
| -------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------- |

| Theoretical     | Course type:    | None                                  | Corequisite:    |
| 64       | Number of hours: | 4                                      | Number of credits: |
|          |             |  Yes                                  | Problem-solving session:   |

**Course syllabus:**

Euclidean space; parametric equations; spatial coordinates; vectors in space; dot product and cross product; equations of lines and planes; quadric surfaces; vector-valued functions and their derivatives; velocity and acceleration; curvature and normal vectors to a curve; multivariable functions; partial and directional derivatives; tangent planes and normal lines; gradient; chain rule for partial derivatives; total differential; double and multiple integrals and their applications to geometric and physical problems; changing the order of integration (without detailed proof); cylindrical and spherical coordinates; vector fields; line integrals; surface integrals; divergence, curl, Laplacian, potential functions; Green’s, divergence, and Stokes’ theorems.
