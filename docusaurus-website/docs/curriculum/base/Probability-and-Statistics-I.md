**Course syllabus:**

Probability: sample space and probability space; laws of probability; probabilistic models; counting rules; conditional probability; independence; and Bayes’ theorem. Random variables: definition of a random variable; discrete, continuous, and mixed random variables; distribution function; probability mass function and density function. Expectation and moments: expectation; expectation of a function of a random variable; properties and applications of expectation; median and mode of a distribution; variance and other measures of dispersion; skewness and symmetry; moments of a random variable. Transformation of a random variable: probability generating function; moment generating function, and their properties and applications. Standard discrete and continuous distributions: Bernoulli, binomial, geometric, hypergeometric, negative binomial, Poisson, uniform (discrete and continuous), exponential, gamma, beta, and normal. Distribution of a function of a random variable; generating random numbers from a given distribution; probabilistic inequalities: Markov, Chebyshev, and Cauchy–Schwarz; joint distribution function.
