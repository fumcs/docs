**Course syllabus:**

Propositional logic and truth tables; sets and basic operations on sets; Russell’s paradox; relations and functions; equivalence relations and partitions; finite, countable, and uncountable sets; cardinal numbers; Cantor’s theorems; Schröder–Bernstein theorems; the Axiom of Choice and its equivalent formulations.
