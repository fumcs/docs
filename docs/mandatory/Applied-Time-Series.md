**Course syllabus:**


- Presenting the objectives of time series analysis with examples from various domains such as meteorology and economic and financial topics; trend, seasonal component, and decomposition of time series; estimation of trends using regression models; smoothing; basic concepts of time series and stochastic processes (mean and variance functions, autocovariance function, autocorrelation and partial autocorrelation, stationarity); white-noise process, random walk, general linear time series, moving-average and autoregressive processes; ARMA and ARIMA models (seasonal and non-seasonal) within the Box–Jenkins framework; unit-root tests, differencing, parameter estimation (method of moments, least squares, and maximum likelihood); forecasting; residual analysis and model diagnostics; model adequacy; autocorrelation tests; normality tests; AIC and BIC criteria for model selection; introduction to conditional heteroscedasticity models; Box–Cox transformations.

- Note: Implementation and practical application in software are part of the syllabus and are carried out at the instructor’s discretion.
