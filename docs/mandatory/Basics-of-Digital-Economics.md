**Course syllabus:**


- The Fourth Industrial Revolution

- The trajectory of the presence and penetration of digital technologies in industry

- The digital revolution

- Digital transformation

- Economic trends and technologies related to the digital economy

- Artificial intelligence / blockchain / Internet of Things / …

- Business models in the digital economy

- The platform revolution

- Fourth-generation electronic commerce 

- Opportunity recognition and ideation in the space of digital businesses

- Design and launch of businesses in the digital economy

- Principles of team formation in the context of digital businesses

- Principles of project management and teamwork in the context of digital businesses 

- Key roles in a business based on the digital economy

- Formulating market entry strategies and marketing principles in digital businesses

- Formulating strategies for attracting, retaining, and developing customers in digital businesses

- Pricing of products and services

- Methods of financing and approaches to presenting to investors and their requirements

- Technology valuation

- Components of the innovation ecosystem based on the digital economy and familiarity with their roles
