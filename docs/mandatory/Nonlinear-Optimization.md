**Course syllabus:**

This course studies optimization problems in which the objective function and/or constraints are nonlinear. Emphasis is placed on understanding optimality conditions, designing algorithms, and applying nonlinear optimization to problems arising in data science, engineering, and operations research.

Topics include: unconstrained optimization in \(\mathbb{R}^n\); gradient and Hessian; necessary and sufficient conditions for local optimality; line search methods; steepest descent; Newton and quasi-Newton methods; trust-region methods; and convergence properties. Constrained optimization: equality and inequality constraints; Lagrange multipliers; Karush–Kuhn–Tucker (KKT) conditions; penalty and barrier methods; projected gradient and related approaches.

Convex optimization is treated as a central and tractable subarea: convex sets and functions; duality; primal–dual methods; and basic algorithms for convex problems. Applications may include parameter estimation, model fitting, portfolio optimization, and machine learning.
