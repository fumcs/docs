**Course syllabus:**

**According to the guidelines for the implementation of skill- and employability-oriented courses, this course is preferably offered within the first six semesters of the continuous bachelor’s program, with the aim of familiarizing students with and enabling them to learn a new profession related to their field of study.**

**The apprenticeship content for each student is determined by the host company or organization and must be approved by the academic department.**
