**Course syllabus:**

This course provides an introduction to deep learning as a subfield of machine learning, focusing on fundamental architectures, training algorithms, and practical considerations. The emphasis is on understanding the behavior of deep models, choosing appropriate architectures, and monitoring and improving their performance.

Topics include: review of multivariate calculus and linear algebra relevant to deep learning; fully connected neural networks; forward and backward propagation; loss functions; gradient-based optimization methods (e.g., SGD, momentum, Adam); initialization schemes; normalization; and regularization techniques such as weight decay and dropout.

Convolutional neural networks (CNNs): convolution and pooling operations; common architectures; applications to image and signal data; practical considerations for training and evaluation. Recurrent and sequence models: motivation for sequence modeling; recurrent neural networks (RNNs); gated architectures such as GRUs and LSTMs; sequence-to-sequence models and attention mechanisms at an introductory level.

The course also covers overfitting and generalization, monitoring training (learning curves, validation metrics), hyperparameter tuning, and the use of modern deep-learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow, including implementation of basic models and experiments.
