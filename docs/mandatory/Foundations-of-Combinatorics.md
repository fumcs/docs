**Course syllabus:**

This course continues the study of combinatorics at a more advanced level, building on the material covered in “Foundation of Combinatorics”. The emphasis is on deeper counting techniques, more sophisticated combinatorial structures, and applications to computer science.

Topics include: advanced applications of the inclusion–exclusion principle; exponential generating functions; recurrence relations and their solution via generating functions; combinatorial identities and bijective proofs; introduction to partially ordered sets and lattices; Möbius inversion on posets; more advanced graph-theoretic topics relevant to combinatorics (such as matchings, connectivity, planarity, and basic extremal problems); and an introduction to probabilistic methods in combinatorics.

Selected topics in design theory, finite geometries, or coding theory may be included at the instructor’s discretion, with emphasis on connections to earlier material and to computer science applications.
