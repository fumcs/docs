**Course syllabus:**

The Mathematics Lab course is designed to strengthen students’ conceptual understanding and computational skills through the use of computer-based tools. The focus is on exploring mathematical ideas experimentally, visualizing concepts, and performing symbolic and numerical computations relevant to other courses in the curriculum.

Topics include: basic use of a scientific programming environment (such as Python with SymPy/NumPy/SciPy, or a similar CAS); symbolic manipulation of expressions; solving equations and systems symbolically and numerically; plotting functions and data; numerical approximation and visualization of limits, derivatives, and integrals; and simple simulations or explorations related to linear algebra and differential equations.

Emphasis is placed on using computation to check conjectures, explore examples and counterexamples, and support problem solving in calculus, linear algebra, and other foundational courses.
