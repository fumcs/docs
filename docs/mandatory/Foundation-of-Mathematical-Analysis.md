**Course syllabus:**

This course provides an introduction to rigorous real analysis in one variable, forming the basis for more advanced courses in analysis, probability, and related fields. The emphasis is on precise definitions, proofs, and the development of mathematical maturity.

Topics include: the real number system and its completeness; sequences and limits; boundedness, convergence, limsup and liminf; series of real numbers, tests for convergence, and power series; continuity and uniform continuity; differentiation and related theorems (mean value theorem, L’Hôpital’s rule, Taylor’s theorem with remainder); Riemann integration, fundamental theorems of calculus, and basic properties of integrable functions; sequences and series of functions, uniform convergence, and interchange of limits, derivatives, and integrals under appropriate conditions.

Throughout the course, proof techniques such as epsilon–delta arguments and mathematical induction are emphasized, and examples are drawn from calculus and elementary differential equations.
