**Course syllabus:**

1- Analysis and evaluation of algorithms (an introduction to complexity)

2- Divide-and-conquer approach and related problems, such as quicksort and mergesort algorithms, and Strassen’s algorithm for multiplication of large matrices

3- Dynamic programming approach and related problems, such as longest common subsequence, sequence alignment, matrix chain multiplication, and optimal search trees

4- Greedy approach and related problems, such as greedy algorithms for scheduling problems, greedy algorithm for the maximum activity-selection problem, and minimum spanning trees

5- Backtracking approach and related problems, such as the N-queens problem and graph coloring

6- Branch-and-bound approach and related problems, such as the knapsack problem

7- Graph algorithms: breadth-first and depth-first search, shortest paths, minimum spanning tree, connected components, and topological sorting

8- Different types of search algorithms and their comparison

9- Introduction to computational complexity and the classes P, NP, NP-complete, and NP-hard
