**Course syllabus:**

- Introduction to data mining and its relationship with statistics, databases, and OLAP; distinction between data mining and traditional statistical analysis; the role of data warehousing and data preprocessing in data mining; basic concepts of knowledge discovery in databases (KDD), including data cleaning, integration, transformation, reduction, and discretization; overview of supervised and unsupervised methods and the main stages of a data mining project, from problem definition and data understanding to model building, evaluation, and deployment.

- Association analysis: market basket analysis, association rules, support, confidence, lift, and other interestingness measures; the Apriori algorithm and its variants; constraint-based association rule mining; introduction to sequential pattern mining and basic algorithms such as GSP, SPADE, and FreeSpan; notions of pattern explosion and frequent pattern compression; overview of motif discovery and matrix profile in time series data.

- Clustering: basic concepts and objectives; similarity and distance measures; partitioning methods such as k-means; hierarchical clustering (agglomerative and divisive); density-based methods (e.g., DBSCAN); grid-based methods (e.g., CLIQUE); evaluation of clustering quality (internal, external, and relative criteria); issues of scalability and high-dimensional data; clustering in large datasets and streaming data.

- Practical implementation of data mining algorithms using tools and libraries (for example, RapidMiner, scikit-learn, or similar environments); end-to-end examples covering preprocessing, model construction, and evaluation on real or realistic datasets.
