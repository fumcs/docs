**Course syllabus:**

This laboratory course is devoted to the use of computational tools for exploring and visualizing mathematical concepts, with an emphasis on experimentation, problem solving, and communication of mathematical ideas.

Topics include: symbolic computation with computer algebra systems; numerical computation and visualization using high-level programming languages (such as Python with NumPy, SymPy, and Matplotlib); visualization of functions, curves, surfaces, and data sets; numerical approximation and solution of algebraic and differential equations; simulation of discrete and continuous systems; and preparation of technical reports and presentations that combine text, formulas, and graphics.

Students work on small projects that integrate mathematical reasoning with computer-based experimentation.
