**Course syllabus:**

- General overview of operating systems. Historical development of operating systems. Process management. Multithreading. CPU scheduling. Concurrency and synchronization management. Deadlock management and algorithms for deadlock detection and resolution. Storage management. Linux systems programming.

**References:**

- Tanenbaum, A. S. (2014). Modern Operating Systems (4th ed.). Pearson.

- Silberschatz, A., Galvin, P. B., & Gagne, G. (2013). Operating System Concepts (9th ed.). John Wiley & Sons.

- Stallings, W. (2015). Operating Systems: Internals and Design Principles (9th ed.). Pearson.
