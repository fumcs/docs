**Course syllabus:**

Basic concepts and methods of digital image processing. Topics include: image acquisition and representation; color spaces; histogram processing; spatial filtering; frequency-domain filtering; edge detection; image segmentation; simple morphological operations; and introductory compression concepts.

**References:**

- Gonzalez, R. C., & Woods, R. E. (2018). Digital Image Processing (4th ed.). Pearson.
