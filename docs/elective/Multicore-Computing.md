**Course syllabus:**

This course studies programming models and architectural issues that arise in multicore and many-core systems, with emphasis on writing correct and efficient parallel programs.

Topics include: overview of multicore and many-core architectures; shared-memory and distributed-memory models; threads and processes; synchronization mechanisms (locks, semaphores, barriers, and atomic operations); memory consistency and cache coherence; parallel patterns (map, reduce, scan, stencil, fork-join, and pipeline); data partitioning and load balancing; performance analysis and profiling; GPU and accelerator programming; and case studies of high-performance applications.

Practical exposure may include programming with POSIX threads, OpenMP, CUDA or similar GPU frameworks, and message-passing libraries such as MPI.
