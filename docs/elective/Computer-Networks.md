**Course syllabus:**

Overview of computer networks and the Internet; layered network architectures; application, transport, network, link, and physical layers. Socket-based network programming; IP packet switching; IP addressing and routing; transport protocols (TCP and UDP); congestion control; name and address resolution (DNS, DHCP, ARP); middleware concepts; switches and bridges; links and link-layer protocols; distance-vector, link-state, and path-vector routing (including BGP); overlay and peer-to-peer networks; multimedia streaming; circuit switching; wireless and mobile networks; and content distribution networks (CDNs).
