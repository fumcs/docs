**Course syllabus:**

This course provides a broad introduction to natural language processing (NLP), focusing on computational methods for analyzing, understanding, and generating human language.

Topics include: basic linguistic concepts (morphology, syntax, semantics, and pragmatics) relevant to NLP; language modeling and word representations; text preprocessing and normalization; n-gram models and smoothing; sequence labeling (part-of-speech tagging, named-entity recognition, and chunking); syntactic parsing; distributional semantics and word embeddings; text classification and sentiment analysis; neural architectures for NLP (recurrent networks, convolutional networks, attention mechanisms, and Transformers); and evaluation methodologies.

The course emphasizes both classical statistical models and modern neural approaches, and includes practical assignments using standard NLP libraries and toolkits.
