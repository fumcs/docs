**Course syllabus:**

- Probability and proof by the probabilistic method. Basic randomized algorithmic techniques. Random permutations and their applications. Lower bounds for randomized algorithms. Randomized data structures. Random walks. Monte Carlo methods. Algebraic methods in randomized algorithms. Randomized graph algorithms. Entropy and information-theoretic tools.

**References:**

- Motwani, R., & Raghavan, P. (1995). Randomized Algorithms. Cambridge University Press.

- Matoušek, J., & Vondrák, J. (2001). The Probabilistic Method [Lecture notes]. Department of Applied Mathematics, Charles University.

- Mitzenmacher, M., & Upfal, E. (2005). Probability and Computing: Randomized Algorithms and Probabilistic Analysis. Cambridge University Press.
