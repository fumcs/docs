**Course syllabus:**

Foundations of graph theory and its applications. Topics include: graphs, subgraphs, degrees, paths, cycles, and connectivity; special classes of graphs such as complete, bipartite, and regular graphs; trees, spanning trees, and minimum spanning trees; Eulerian and Hamiltonian graphs; planar graphs; colorings and chromatic number; matchings and factors; and network flows at an introductory level.

Applications may be drawn from network design, scheduling, data structures, and algorithmic problems.
