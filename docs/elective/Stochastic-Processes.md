**Course syllabus:**

Review of probability-generating functions and random sums of random variables. Basic definitions and concepts of stochastic processes: finite-dimensional distributions; stationarity; processes with identically distributed samples; processes with independent samples; Markov property. Bernoulli and binomial processes and their properties. Counting processes. Random-walk processes: properties and the gambler’s ruin problem.

Poisson processes: definition and properties; decomposition of a Poisson process; distribution of event times and inter-arrival times; restarted Poisson processes; relationships with exponential, binomial, geometric, uniform, and beta distributions; conditional distribution of inter-arrival times and connections with order statistics of the uniform distribution. Compound Poisson processes and applications in renewal theory and risk theory. Non-homogeneous Poisson processes and their properties. Review of birth–death processes.

Markov chains: transition functions; one-step and multi-step transition matrices; Chapman–Kolmogorov equations; initial distributions; absolute and joint probabilities; decomposition of the state space; irreducible and ergodic chains; transient and recurrent states; positive and null recurrent states; expected number of visits to a recurrent state; expected first-passage times; absorption probabilities. Stationary distributions, their properties, and methods of computation. Estimation of the transition matrix.
