**Course syllabus:**

Introduction to business intelligence (BI) and its role in decision support. Data warehousing and ETL (extract, transform, load) processes; basics of data integration; introduction to OLAP; dashboards and reporting; key performance indicators (KPIs); and self-service BI concepts.

Students are introduced to one or more BI tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau) for building simple dashboards and reports based on transactional and aggregated data.

**References:**

- Vercellis, C. (2011). Business Intelligence: Data Mining and Optimization for Decision Making. Wiley.

- Grossmann, W., & Rinderle-Ma, S. (2015). Fundamentals of Business Intelligence. Springer.

- Sauter, V. L. (2014). Decision Support Systems for Business Intelligence. Wiley.
