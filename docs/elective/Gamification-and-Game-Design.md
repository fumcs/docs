**Course syllabus:**

This course covers principles of game design and gamification. Topics include: game mechanics and dynamics; player experience and motivation; game balance and feedback systems; game loops; and the use of game elements in non-game contexts (gamification) to drive engagement and behavior change.

Students are introduced to simple tools and frameworks for prototyping games and gamified applications.

**References:**

- Schell, J. (2015). The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses (2nd ed.). CRC Press.
