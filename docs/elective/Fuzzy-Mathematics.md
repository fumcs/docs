**Course syllabus:**

Introduction to fuzzy set theory and its applications. Topics include: basic concepts of fuzzy sets; membership functions; fuzzy operations; fuzzy relations; fuzzy numbers; fuzzy logic; and applications in decision-making, control, and approximate reasoning.

**References:**

- Klir, G. J., & Yuan, B. (1995). Fuzzy Sets and Fuzzy Logic: Theory and Applications. Prentice Hall.
