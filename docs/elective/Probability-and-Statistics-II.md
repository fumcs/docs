**Course syllabus:**

- Joint distributions: joint probability mass, density, and distribution functions; independent random variables; covariance and correlation coefficient; examples of important joint distributions. Conditional distributions: discrete and continuous conditional distributions; applications; conditional expectation and its uses (including the law of total expectation) and conditional variance. Distributions of functions of random variables: distribution-function method; change-of-variable methods for two and higher dimensions; moment-generating functions; distribution of sums of random variables; random sums and the expected value of random sums. Central limit theorem; normal approximation to the binomial distribution; normal approximation to the Poisson distribution. Descriptive statistics: samples and populations; summary statistics; graphical representations. Random samples; sampling distribution of the sample mean; basic sampling distributions (Student’s t, chi-square, and F). Point and interval estimation of unknown parameters; confidence intervals for large samples. Introduction to hypothesis testing: tests of simple hypotheses; one-sided and two-sided tests; tests on the variance of a normal population; tests on means and proportions for large samples; inference about two means; inference using paired samples; tests on means and proportions for two populations with large samples.
