**Course syllabus:**

Introduction to basic structures of abstract algebra with an emphasis on concepts relevant to computer science. Topics include: groups, subgroups, cyclic groups, and permutation groups; homomorphisms and quotient structures; rings and fields at an elementary level; and examples connecting algebraic structures to coding theory and cryptography.

**References:**

- Fraleigh, J. B. (2002). A First Course in Abstract Algebra (7th ed.). Addison-Wesley.

- Herstein, I. N. (1996). Abstract Algebra (3rd ed.). Prentice Hall.
