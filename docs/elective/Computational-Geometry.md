**Course syllabus:**

Basic geometric primitives (points, lines, segments, polygons); orientation and intersection tests; convex hulls; line segment intersection; point location; range searching; Voronoi diagrams and Delaunay triangulations; sweep-line algorithms; and geometric data structures. Emphasis is placed on algorithmic design and analysis, and applications in graphics, robotics, GIS, and related fields.

**References:**

- de Berg, M., Cheong, O., van Kreveld, M., & Overmars, M. (2008). Computational Geometry: Algorithms and Applications (3rd ed.). Springer.

- Preparata, F. P., & Shamos, M. I. (1985). Computational Geometry: An Introduction. Springer.
