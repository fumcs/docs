**Course syllabus:**

This course studies the fundamental concepts and methods of linear algebra with an emphasis on their use in computer science, numerical analysis, and data science.

Topics include: systems of linear equations and Gaussian elimination; vector spaces and subspaces; linear independence, basis, and dimension; linear transformations and matrices; rank and nullity; determinants; eigenvalues and eigenvectors; diagonalization and Jordan forms; inner product spaces, orthogonality, and orthogonal projections; least-squares problems; and introductory numerical issues related to conditioning and stability.

Applications may include Markov chains, graphs and networks, principal component analysis, and linear models in machine learning.
