**Course syllabus:**

Introduction to modern cryptography; classical ciphers and their cryptanalysis; basic notions of security; symmetric-key cryptography (block ciphers, stream ciphers, modes of operation); public-key cryptography (RSA and related schemes, Diffie–Hellman key exchange); hash functions and message authentication codes; digital signatures; and an introduction to cryptographic protocols.

**References:**

- Katz, J., & Lindell, Y. (2007). Introduction to Modern Cryptography. CRC Press.

- Goldreich, O. (2001). Foundations of Cryptography, Volume 1: Basic Tools. Cambridge University Press.

- Goldreich, O. (2004). Foundations of Cryptography, Volume 2: Basic Applications. Cambridge University Press.

- Menezes, A. J., van Oorschot, P. C., & Vanstone, S. A. (1996). Handbook of Applied Cryptography. CRC Press.
