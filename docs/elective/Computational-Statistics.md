**Course syllabus:**

This course covers numerical and simulation-based techniques for statistical inference.

Topics include: random number generation and transformation; Monte Carlo integration; bootstrap methods; permutation tests; Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods at an introductory level; and applications of computational statistics in data analysis and machine learning.

**References:**

- Robert, C. P., & Casella, G. (2010). Introducing Monte Carlo Methods with R. Springer.

- Efron, B., & Tibshirani, R. J. (1994). An Introduction to the Bootstrap. CRC Press.
