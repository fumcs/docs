**Course syllabus:**

This course provides an introduction to Fourier analysis and wavelet analysis with an emphasis on concepts and methods that are important in applied mathematics, signal processing, and data analysis.

Topics include: orthogonal systems and Fourier series; convergence and approximation properties; the Fourier transform on the real line; basic properties in the time and frequency domains; discrete-time Fourier analysis; multiresolution analysis; construction of orthonormal wavelet bases; discrete wavelet transform and filter banks; and applications of wavelets and Fourier methods to data compression, denoising, feature extraction, and numerical solution of differential equations.

Where appropriate, examples and computational experiments are used to illustrate the theory and to highlight numerical aspects of Fourier and wavelet methods.
